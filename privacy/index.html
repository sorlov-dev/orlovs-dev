Privacy Policy

This policy covers the website orlovs.dev only. Each OSV Labs application has its own privacy policy, published on that application's own website.

Last updated: 13.09.2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by OSV Labs, the trading name of ORLOV, SERGEI, an independent Android app studio registered in Argentina. Business address and registration details are shown in the footer of every page and on the Contact page. For any question about this policy, write to contact@orlovs.dev.

2. What this website collects

orlovs.dev is a static website. It has no user accounts, no login, no contact form and no advertising. We do not ask you for personal data on this website, and we do not sell or share personal data with anyone for marketing purposes.

We do not run any analytics service on this website. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not track you across other websites.

3. Cookies and local storage

This website sets no cookies of its own. It stores a single value in your browser's local storage, named theme, which remembers whether you chose the light or the dark version of the site. This value stays in your browser, is never sent to us, and can be removed at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

4. Hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). It loads its typeface from Google Fonts (Google LLC) and its icons from the Cloudflare cdnjs service (Cloudflare, Inc.). To deliver these files, those providers necessarily receive your IP address, the time of the request and basic technical details of your browser, and may keep them in their own server logs under their own privacy policies. We do not receive those logs and have no access to them.

Links to activetrend.app, GitHub, LinkedIn and Telegram lead to external websites that are governed by their own privacy policies.

5. If you write to us

When you send an email to contact@orlovs.dev, we receive your email address, your name if you give it, and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it only to answer you and to keep a record of the matter you raised. We keep such correspondence for as long as it is needed for support, accounting or legal reasons, and then delete it.

6. Your rights

Under Argentine Law 25.326 on the protection of personal data, and under the GDPR where it applies to you, you may ask us what personal data of yours we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to contact@orlovs.dev and we will answer. In Argentina, the supervisory authority is the Agencia de Acceso a la Informacion Publica.

7. Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children through it.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
